✨ Enhance with ambient memory? (optional)

Deeper Notebook can remember what you were working on without you having to tell it. Ask things like:

  • "What was the bug in that file?"
  • "Summarize the article I just read."
  • "Continue what I was doing."

This uses OpenChronicle (MIT), a separate free app that reads your screen via macOS accessibility to build local-only memory. Nothing leaves your machine.
